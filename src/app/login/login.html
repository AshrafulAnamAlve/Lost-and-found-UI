Why use Lost & Found BUBT?

How your report is matched, and how your details are kept safe

Smart matching

Reports are compared by meaning, not only by exact words, so a "notebook computer" can still match a "laptop".

Instant results

As soon as you submit a report, it is checked against every report on the other side and the five closest matches are shown to you.

Verified contact details

Contact details come from your account profile, so every report carries a real name and a phone number that works.

Photo recognition

Our image model identifies the item in your photo, suggests the right category, and warns you if the photo and the category do not match.
