Why Lost & Found BUBT?

Exactly what the system does with your report — nothing more claimed than it does

Two-Layer Matching

Word-level rules score the obvious overlap, and a sentence-embedding model reads the meaning — so a “notebook computer” can still match a “laptop”.

Matches On Submit

The moment a report is filed it is scored against every item on the other side, and the five strongest candidates come back with the response.

One Profile, One Identity

Contact details are taken from your account, never retyped per report — so nobody posts under a made-up name or an unreachable number.

The Photo Is Checked

An image classifier names what it sees in your photo, fills in the category for you, and warns you when the picture disagrees with the category you picked.
